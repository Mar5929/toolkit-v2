Here is what is agreed for the entry screen so far. All of it is in the work item WI-031 (Transaction entry), which is still in Refining Requirements, and its requirements are not approved yet. Nothing about the screen's layout or colors is recorded. I did not open the Figma file, so I cannot say what Nadia's design shows.

**Agreed**

- **Goal:** a user logs a transaction on the iPhone in a few taps and sees it in the day's list (WI-031, Q-01). The day's list is shown below the entry screen (`system/glossary.md`).
- **Required fields:** amount, date, and category. Notes are optional (WI-031, Q-04, R-01).
- **Missing field:** the app blocks the save and shows a message that names the missing field (WI-031, R-01).
- **Speed:** logging a transaction takes under 10 seconds from opening the app (WI-031, Q-03, R-02).
- **When the screen opens:** the amount field is already selected and the date holds today (WI-031, R-02).
- **Categories for the first version:** Groceries, Rent, Transport, Eating out, Other (WI-031, Notes).
- **iPhone only**, on iOS 17 and newer (WI-031, Q-02; DEC-001).

**Still open, all waiting on you**

- **Q-05:** can a user change or delete a transaction after saving it? Recommended: yes to both, because people mistype amounts.
- **Q-06:** which currencies must the first version support? Recommended: US dollars only.
- **DEC-002:** do people who live outside the United States join the first beta? This affects Q-06. Its check-again date is today.

**Not agreed, only an idea:** opening the app on the entry screen instead of the day's list (WI-031, Notes).
