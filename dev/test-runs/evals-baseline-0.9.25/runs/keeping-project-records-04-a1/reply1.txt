A transaction needs an amount, a date, and a category before it is saved and appears on the tape. Notes are optional (WI-031, Requirement 1, from your answer to Q-04 on 2026-09-30).

- **Missing field:** if any of the three is missing, the app blocks the save and shows a message that names the missing field.
- **Date:** when the entry screen opens, the date already holds today and the amount field is selected (WI-031, Requirement 2). In practice the user fills in the amount and the category.
- **Still open:** which currencies a user may enter is not decided (Q-06 in WI-031, which waits on your answer).
