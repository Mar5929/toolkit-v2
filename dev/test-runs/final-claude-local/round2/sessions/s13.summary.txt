**WI-001 result: build the slug command**

It works like this: `node slug.js "<title>"` prints a URL slug. "Hello World" becomes `hello-world`, "Café Déjà Vu" becomes `cafe-deja-vu`, and "Don't Stop" becomes `dont-stop`. If you give it no title, or a title with no letters or digits, it prints a usage message and exits with an error. `--help` prints the same usage message.
