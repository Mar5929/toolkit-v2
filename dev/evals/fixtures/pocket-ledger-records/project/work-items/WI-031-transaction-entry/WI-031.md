## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Required fields

- **Original verbatim requirement:** "Amount, date, category. Notes are optional." (Mike, 2026-09-30, Q-04)
- **Goal:** Every saved transaction holds what the reports group by.

**Functional Requirements**

1. The app saves a transaction only when it has an amount, a date, and a category.
2. Notes are optional.
3. Which currencies a user may enter is not decided yet (Q-06).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. When the amount, the date, or the category is missing, the app blocks the save and shows a message that names the missing field.
2. A transaction with empty notes saves.

### Requirement 2: Fast entry

- **Original verbatim requirement:** "Under 10 seconds. That is the whole point." (Mike, 2026-09-29, Q-03)
- **Goal:** Logging a transaction is fast enough that a user keeps doing it.

**Functional Requirements**

1. From opening the app, a user can log a transaction in under 10 seconds.
2. When the entry screen opens, the amount field is already selected and the date holds today.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. A tester logs 5 transactions, each starting from app launch. Each one takes under 10 seconds.

### Requirement 3: Editing and deleting after saving

- **Goal:** A user can correct a transaction that was saved with a mistake.
