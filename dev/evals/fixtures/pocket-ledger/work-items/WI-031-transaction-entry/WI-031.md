## Requirements

**Overall status:** Refining  
**Finalization rule:** Mark the overall requirements Finalized only when the agreed goal, every active requirement, and acceptance have been reviewed and approved by the owner. Record who approved them and when.

### Starting request

"I want people to be able to log a transaction fast on their phone. Amount, what it was for, done."

### Agreed goal

A user can log a money transaction on the iPhone in a few taps and see it in the day's list.

### Active requirements

#### R-01 — Required fields

**Status:** Refining  
**Applies to:** Every transaction a user logs.  
**Required behavior:** A transaction cannot be saved without an amount, a date, and a category. Notes are optional.  
**Boundaries and exceptions:** Which currencies are allowed is open (Q-06).  
**Acceptance check:** Saving with the amount, the date, or the category missing is blocked with a message that names the missing field. Saving with empty notes works.  
**Decision record:** Q-04, answered 2026-09-30.  
**Open questions:** Q-06.

#### R-02 — Fast entry

**Status:** Refining  
**Applies to:** A user opening the app to log a transaction.  
**Required behavior:** From opening the app, a user can log a transaction in under 10 seconds. The amount field is already selected and the date is filled in with today.  
**Boundaries and exceptions:** None identified.  
**Acceptance check:** A tester logs 5 transactions starting from app launch. Each takes under 10 seconds.  
**Decision record:** Q-03, answered 2026-09-29.  
**Open questions:** None.

#### R-03 — Editing and deleting after saving

**Status:** Refining  
**Applies to:** A user who wants to change or remove a saved transaction.  
**Required behavior:** Not decided yet (Q-05).  
**Boundaries and exceptions:** Not decided yet.  
**Acceptance check:** Not decided yet.  
**Decision record:** Waiting on Q-05.  
**Open questions:** Q-05.
