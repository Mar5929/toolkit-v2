How the records relate

The storage choice changes where records live. Their purposes guide how an agent uses them.

Active work

Requirements, proposals, working designs, tasks, and progress.

Chosen work tracker
→

Approved decisions

The owner’s agreement and its supporting evidence remain traceable.

Work records or existing decision ledger
→

Product Wiki

Eligible finalized product intent updates maintained documents.

Existing promotion and filing policies apply

Reference supplies evidence

Research, outside documentation, and dated current-system findings support work and product records. Original received material remains in sources/.

Memory preserves lasting context

Qualifying lessons and context help future sessions. Memory points to authoritative records instead of becoming another copy of the product specification.

This relationship explains the intended distinction. TKV-70 changes local names and paths; it does not replace provider-specific approval, promotion, or memory rules. Approved product intent and verified implementation remain separate.

How an existing project adopts the layout

1

Review the populated records

Identify the project’s authoritative documents, owner customizations, selected modules, and any content already at the destination paths.

2

Move records before copying templates

Move the populated old folders into the approved new homes. Review collisions with the owner; preserve content and keep originals recoverable.

3

Update the routes together

Update links, guide paths, project maps, setup and update guidance. Preserve document IDs, guide pairs, file meanings, and owner-owned files.

4

Check the result and update ignored records

Verify setup, update, and retrieval workflows. After the project’s tracked layout branch merges, apply its approved link corrections to ignored records without staging them. Other projects adopt the change through their own reviewed “update the toolkit” work.

This delivery does not migrate another project or change live Notion, GitHub, or Hindsight configuration. Active TKV-62 work is accounted for in the isolated worktree.

Broader findings for follow-up

These questions support consistent methodology across providers. They are outside the local layout implementation.

Follow-up · document form

Readable product documentation and numbered rules

Local system documents emphasize numbered rules. Product explanations, workflows, and diagrams may need a broader form while preserving useful IDs and traceability.

Follow-up · promotion

When finalized work becomes product knowledge

Compare local promotion criteria with the Notion Product Wiki workflow. Lasting product behavior may deserve a maintained record even when it arose from one work item.

Follow-up · ownership

One authoritative home for each subject

Confirm that local and hosted wiki choices avoid competing documents. Keep approved intent, current findings, work status, and lasting lessons distinguishable.

Follow-up · maintenance

Later changes update living records

Compare how each provider handles corrections, supersession, diagrams, and supporting decision links. Preserve the shared methodology while adapting storage operations.
