# What setup may do in the owner's Notion

This file holds the limits on what setup does in the owner's Notion workspace.

- Make nothing in the owner's real Notion workspace without the owner's yes. Before you make anything, tell the owner what will be made: this project's pages in the owner's Notion, from the "Project" template in the owner's Projects database. For Notion memory, include the librarian entry, references and project context pages in that preview. The owner's yes covers only what you named. An approved batch in "update the toolkit" may cover the listed writes and their confirmed destination; ask again before changing either.
- Make only the writes the owner approved in the preview. Reading the owner's Notion to find the Projects database, check the template, and verify what setup made needs no separate yes.
- Every Notion project is a copy of the "Project" template in the owner's Projects database. The template is the owner's. When it does not exist, stop and tell the owner: the owner makes it in the Notion app, and the toolkit does not build it.
- When the owner says this project is a test, make its Notion pages only under the test page the owner names. Never make them under the owner's real pages unless the owner says so.
