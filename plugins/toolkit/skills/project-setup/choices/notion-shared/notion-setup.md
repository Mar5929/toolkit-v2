# Notion: the steps that work items and memory share

These are the Notion steps that the Notion work items choice and the Notion memory choice share. When work items and memory are both in Notion, do each step once. The job is done when the owner is signed in, the project's Notion pages were made only after the owner's yes, and `PROJECT.md` holds the project's Notion links and IDs.

## Steps

Do step 1 right after the owner picks Notion, before the next interview question. Do steps 2 to 5 when the owner's sign-in is done and the owner has answered where work items and memory are kept and whether this is a Salesforce project.

1. **Ask the owner for the Notion sign-in, and check it.** Ask as soon as the owner picks Notion, so that the owner can sign in while the interview goes on. [reference/notion-sign-in.md](reference/notion-sign-in.md) says what the owner does, and what to do when `ntn` is not installed or a call cannot reach a page.
2. **Tell the owner what will be made in Notion, and get the owner's yes.** [reference/what-setup-may-do-in-the-owners-notion.md](reference/what-setup-may-do-in-the-owners-notion.md) gives what to tell the owner, what the yes covers, what to do when the owner has no "Project" template, and what to do for a test project.
3. **Confirm which Projects database gets the project's row.** [reference/where-the-notion-project-page-goes.md](reference/where-the-notion-project-page-goes.md) gives the call that finds the database, the question, and what to do when the owner names a page or has no Projects database.
4. **Make the project's pages from the owner's "Project" template.** Follow [reference/notion-project-pages.md](reference/notion-project-pages.md), "A new project". It makes the project page, picks the ID prefix when work items are in Notion, removes the part this project keeps elsewhere, and runs [reference/notion-memory-pages.md](reference/notion-memory-pages.md) when memory is in Notion.
5. **Write the links and IDs in `PROJECT.md`, and read them back.** [reference/where-the-notion-links-and-ids-go.md](reference/where-the-notion-links-and-ids-go.md) says where each line goes.

A project that has work items or memory in Notion and adds the other one follows [reference/notion-project-pages.md](reference/notion-project-pages.md), "Adding work items or memory later". "Update the toolkit" follows [reference/updating-a-notion-project.md](reference/updating-a-notion-project.md).
