# Memory in Notion

Follow these steps when the owner answers "Notion" to question 6 of the guide, [SKILL.md](../../../SKILL.md). The job is done when the Memory page and the "Current state" page exist in the owner's Notion, the Notion files are in the project, `PROJECT.md` records the choice and the ids, and the session-start command prints the project's Current State, which is the project's one short page of what is true now.

## Steps

Do steps 1 to 4 right after the answer, before the next question of the guide. Do step 5 when the owner's sign-in is done and the owner has answered questions 5, 6, and 7 of the guide. Do steps 6 to 10 when setup finishes, in Step 6 of the guide. Steps 4 and 5 send you to [choices/notion-shared/notion-setup.md](../../notion-shared/notion-setup.md), which holds the sign-in and the Notion steps once, because Notion work items use the same ones.

1. **Tell the owner how the toolkit sets up memory in Notion by default, and ask whether that is what the owner wants.** [reference/notion-memory-default.md](reference/notion-memory-default.md) holds the default. [SKILL.md](../../../SKILL.md), "Each choice has questions of its own", gives the words of the question and what to ask after a no.
2. **Copy the choice's files into the project.** Three folders hold them at the paths a project has them. Copy `choices/memory/shared-by-local-and-notion/files-to-add/` first and `choices/memory/notion/files-to-add/` after it, and copy `choices/notion-shared/files-to-add/`. [reference/what-notion-memory-adds-to-a-project.md](reference/what-notion-memory-adds-to-a-project.md) lists them, and [reference/placing-files.md](../../../reference/placing-files.md) gives the copy command and what to do with a file that the project already has.
3. **Change the session-start line in the settings files, and write `notion` on the Memory line of "Toolkit settings" in `PROJECT.md`.** [reference/what-notion-memory-adds-to-a-project.md](reference/what-notion-memory-adds-to-a-project.md) gives each change and the place where it goes. The "Notion memory" line holds ids, so step 5 writes it when the ids are known.
4. **Ask the owner to sign in to Notion.** Do step 1 of [choices/notion-shared/notion-setup.md](../../notion-shared/notion-setup.md). The owner can sign in while the interview goes on.
5. **Make the project's pages in Notion, after the owner's yes.** Do steps 2 to 5 of [choices/notion-shared/notion-setup.md](../../notion-shared/notion-setup.md), in order.
   - Those steps ask this choice's one question when the project has no page in Notion yet. The question is where the project's page goes: which Projects database gets the project's row.
   - Those steps remove the part of the page that this project keeps elsewhere and fill the fields of the project's row, so they need the owner's answers to question 5, where work items are kept, and question 7, Salesforce.
   - Those steps also write the first lines of the "Current state" page, write the ids in `PROJECT.md`, and put the id of the "Current state" page in the settings files.
6. **Bring the project's Current State up to date.** The project's Current State is the "Current state" page in Notion, and step 5 wrote its first lines when it made the page. Bring them up to date with what [reference/finishing.md](../../../reference/finishing.md), "The project's Current State", says.
7. **When work items are not in Notion, write the project's outcome in the project's row in Notion.** Write the owner's sentence from question 2 in the "Desired Outcome" field of the project's row, and read the row back. [reference/finishing.md](../../../reference/finishing.md), "Fill the prompts", gives the rules for the sentence and the other places where it goes.
8. **Check that the session-start command prints the project's Current State, and check the project's pages in Notion.** `node .claude/hooks/session-start.mjs --memory notion --page <Current state page id> </dev/null` prints the Current State. Then do the check that starts "For a new project" in [choices/notion-shared/reference/notion-project-pages.md](../../notion-shared/reference/notion-project-pages.md), "Checks after any of these". [reference/finishing.md](../../../reference/finishing.md), "Final checks", says how to run a check and what to do when one fails.
9. **Tell the owner the owner's own steps for Notion.** [choices/notion-shared/reference/notion-sign-in.md](../../notion-shared/reference/notion-sign-in.md) gives them. [reference/finishing.md](../../../reference/finishing.md), "The owner's steps", says how to tell the owner a step.
10. **When work items are not in Notion, list the "Project brief" prompts of the project's page in the report.** The prompts are Overview/Context, Goals, and Success criteria. They stay until the owner gives them, so list them with the prompts that are left. [reference/finishing.md](../../../reference/finishing.md), "The report", says where those prompts go in the report.

When a step does not work as expected, open [reference/notion-memory-known-problems.md](reference/notion-memory-known-problems.md).

"Check the setup", "update the toolkit", and a change of where memory is kept use the same steps: steps 2 and 3 give the folders and the lines that this choice adds, and step 8 gives its checks. An update changes nothing in Notion, as the closing lines of [choices/notion-shared/notion-setup.md](../../notion-shared/notion-setup.md) say. [reference/update.md](../../../reference/update.md) says how each of those jobs works.
