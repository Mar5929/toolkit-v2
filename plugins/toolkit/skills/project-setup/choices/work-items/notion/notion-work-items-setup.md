# Work items in Notion

Follow these steps when the owner chooses to keep work items in Notion. The job is done when the project's page and its databases exist in the owner's Notion, the Notion files are in the project, and `PROJECT.md` records the choice, the ID prefix, and the links.

## Steps

Do steps 1 to 4 right after the answer, before the next interview question. Do step 5 when the owner's sign-in is done and the owner has answered where memory is kept and whether this is a Salesforce project. Do steps 6 to 10 when setup finishes. [choices/notion-shared/notion-setup.md](../../notion-shared/notion-setup.md) holds the sign-in and the Notion steps once, because Notion memory uses the same ones.

1. **Tell the owner how the toolkit sets up work items in Notion by default, and ask whether that is what the owner wants.** [reference/notion-work-items-default.md](reference/notion-work-items-default.md) holds the default.
2. **Copy the choice's files into the project.** [reference/what-notion-work-items-add-to-a-project.md](reference/what-notion-work-items-add-to-a-project.md) lists them.
3. **Write `notion` on the Tracker line of "Toolkit settings" in `PROJECT.md`.** The choice's two other lines hold the ID prefix and the Notion links, so step 5 writes them when those values are known.
4. **Ask the owner to sign in to Notion**, as the sign-in step of [notion-setup.md](../../notion-shared/notion-setup.md) says. The owner can sign in while the interview goes on.
5. **Make the project's pages in Notion, after the owner's yes**, as the remaining steps of [notion-setup.md](../../notion-shared/notion-setup.md) say. Those steps ask where the project's page goes, pick the ID prefix, remove the part of the page that this project keeps elsewhere, fill the project's row, and write the prefix, the page link, and the database IDs in `PROJECT.md`.
6. **Write the project's outcome in the project's row in Notion.** Write the owner's sentence from question 2 in the "Desired Outcome" field of the project's row, and read the row back.
7. **Write the roadmap as rows of the Phases database.** Write the project's phases from question 11. The project's `managing-work-items` skill says how, in `references/work-tracker.md`.
8. **Check the project's page in Notion.** Run the checks in [notion-project-pages.md](../../notion-shared/reference/notion-project-pages.md), "Checks after any of these".
9. **Tell the owner the owner's own steps for Notion.** [notion-sign-in.md](../../notion-shared/reference/notion-sign-in.md) gives them.
10. **List the "Project brief" prompts of the project's page in the report.** The prompts are Overview/Context, Goals, and Success criteria. They stay until the owner gives them.

For "update the toolkit", follow [updating-a-notion-project.md](../../notion-shared/reference/updating-a-notion-project.md). An update changes Notion only within an approved batch or another explicit owner instruction.
